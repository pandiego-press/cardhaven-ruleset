# Tide-Stalkers (D&D 5e)

Medium Humanoid, Unaligned
- Armor Class 13 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 16 (3d8+3)
- Speed 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
- STR 13 (+1), DEX 15 (+2), CON 12 (+1), INT 7 (-2), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 8 (-1)
- Challenge 1/4 (50 XP)
- **Ambush.** The stalker has advantage on attack rolls against a creature that hasn't taken a turn yet in the combat.
- **Coral Spear.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.
