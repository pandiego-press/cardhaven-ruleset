# Bramble Warden (D&D 5e)

Huge Plant, Unaligned
- Armor Class 15 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 97 (13d10+26)
- Speed 20 ft.
- STR 19 (+4), DEX 8 (-1), CON 15 (+2), INT 4 (-3), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 6 (-2)
- Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)
- **Entangling Roots (Recharge 5-6).** One creature the Warden can see within 30 feet must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw or become restrained by roots until it escapes with a successful Strength (Athletics) check against the same DC.
- **Thorned Bough.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10+4) piercing damage.
