# Bramble Stalker (D&D 5e)

Small Plant, Unaligned
- Armor Class 12 (bark hide)
- Hit Points 9 (2d6+2)
- Speed 20 ft.
- STR 10 (+0), DEX 12 (+1), CON 12 (+1), INT 2 (-4), WIS 8 (-1), CHA 3 (-4)
- Challenge 1/8 (25 XP)
- **Thorny Hide.** A creature that hits the stalker with a melee attack takes 1 piercing damage.
- **Thorn Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d4) piercing damage.
