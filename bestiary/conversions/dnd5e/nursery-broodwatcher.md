# Nursery Broodwatcher (D&D 5e)

Large Beast, Unaligned
- Armor Class 15 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 58 (9d10+9)
- Speed 30 ft.
- STR 18 (+4), DEX 10 (+0), CON 13 (+1), INT 2 (-4), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 6 (-2)
- Challenge 3 (700 XP)
- **Enrage.** When reduced to half its hit points or fewer, the broodwatcher gains a +2 bonus to damage rolls and its speed increases by 10 feet for the rest of the encounter. Its chitin visibly darkens to a deeper red when this triggers.
- **Crushing Charge.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning damage. This attack ignores any AC bonus the target gains from a shield.
