# Razor-Beetle Alpha (D&D 5e)

Large Beast, Unaligned
- Armor Class 15 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 36 (8d8)
- Speed 30 ft.
- STR 17 (+3), DEX 10 (+0), CON 14 (+2), INT 2 (-4), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 6 (-2)
- Challenge 2 (450 XP)
- **Multifaceted Eyes.** The alpha has advantage on saving throws against being blinded.
- **Multiattack.** The alpha makes a Horn Charge and a Mandible Bite attack.
- **Horn Charge.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6+3) piercing damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away.
- **Mandible Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8+3) piercing damage.
- **Shock Discharge (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature within 10 feet of the alpha must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 8 (2d6+1) lightning damage on a failure. If the alpha has already been struck squarely in the jaw this turn (GM discretion, e.g. following a charged shield attack), it is stunned until the end of its next turn instead of using this action.
