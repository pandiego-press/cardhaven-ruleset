# Vein Matriarch (D&D 5e)

Huge Plant, Unaligned
- Armor Class 16 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 152 (16d12+48)
- Speed 10 ft.
- STR 20 (+5), DEX 8 (-1), CON 17 (+3), INT 6 (-2), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 9 (-1)
- Challenge 8 (3,900 XP)
- **Regenerating Growth.** At the start of its turn, the Matriarch regains 10 HP if it has at least 1 HP.
- **Multiattack.** The Matriarch makes three Thorned Vine attacks.
- **Thorned Vine.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) piercing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15).
