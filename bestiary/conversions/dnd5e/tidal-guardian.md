# Tidal Guardian (D&D 5e)

Huge Construct, Unaligned
- Armor Class 16 (natural armor)
- Hit Points 115 (11d12+44)
- Speed 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
- STR 19 (+4), DEX 10 (+0), CON 18 (+4), INT 8 (-1), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 10 (+0)
- Challenge 6 (2,300 XP)
- **Two-Phase Combatant.** When the Guardian is reduced to half its hit points or fewer, it enters Phase 2 for the rest of the encounter: it gains an additional action on each of its turns, and its Guardian Strike deals an extra 1d8 damage.
- **Guardian Strike.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10+4) bludgeoning damage.
