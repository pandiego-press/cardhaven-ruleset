# Crag-Lord (D&D 5e)

Large Humanoid, Unaligned
- Armor Class 15 (natural armor, hide)
- Hit Points 78 (12d10+12)
- Speed 30 ft.
- STR 18 (+4), DEX 10 (+0), CON 13 (+1), INT 10 (+0), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 10 (+0)
- Challenge 4 (1,100 XP)
- **Rare Spawn.** The Crag-Lord is a tougher variant of its kind and has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.
- **Multiattack.** The Crag-Lord makes two Pickaxe Strike attacks.
- **Pickaxe Strike.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) piercing damage.
- **Charging Tackle.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is knocked prone.
- **Sunder (Recharge 5-6).** The Crag-Lord telegraphs an overhead pickaxe swing. Each creature in a 10-foot radius must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 20 (3d10+4) bludgeoning damage and being stunned until the end of the Crag-Lord's next turn on a failure, or half damage and no stun on a success.
