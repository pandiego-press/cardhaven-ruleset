# Cardhaven Compatible

The "Cardhaven Compatible" badge indicates that a third-party product uses the Cardhaven Ruleset and meets our compatibility standards.

## Requirements

To use the Cardhaven Compatible badge your product must:

1. Use the Cardhaven Ruleset as documented in this repository without breaking changes to the core Triple Bind economy, card slot system, or stat derivation formulas
2. Include the attribution line: "Uses the Cardhaven Ruleset by M.A. Lionmayne, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0"
3. Release any rule modifications under CC BY-SA 4.0
4. NOT use the Cardhaven name, world, characters, or lore without a separate agreement with Pandiego Press
5. NOT imply official endorsement by M.A. Lionmayne or Pandiego Press

## How to Apply

Submit a compatibility request via GitHub Issues with a link to your product and confirmation that you meet the above requirements. Approved products will be listed in COMPATIBLE-PRODUCTS.md.

## Badge Usage

The Cardhaven Compatible logo may be used in approved products in its original form only — no modifications to the logo are permitted. Logo files are available in [/assets/badge/](assets/badge/).
