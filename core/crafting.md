# Crafting

## Shards

Shards have no rarity tier — there is only one Shard type. Every
Shard, regardless of source, is tri-purpose:
- Economy: spend as currency
- Crafting: use as fuel toward a Core's rarity threshold
- Progression: consume for XP via Shard Cultivation

Rarity lives on the **Core** (the crafting blueprint) and on the
resulting **Card** — never on the Shard itself.

## Factory Slots

Every card rarity tier has a dedicated Factory Slot, but the slot
holds a **Core**, not a shard stockpile:
- [Common Card Factory Slot]
- [Uncommon Card Factory Slot]
- [Rare Card Factory Slot]
- [Legendary Card Factory Slot]
- [Mythic Card Factory Slot]

A Core placed in a Factory Slot accumulates generic Shard fuel toward
its rarity threshold (see Crafting Costs by Card Rarity below). Shards
themselves remain loose in inventory until spent — the player
manually allocates them across Economy, Crafting, or Progression at
the point of use.

A Core can be withdrawn from its Factory Slot at any time without
losing accumulated Shard fuel; the slot is an organizational tool,
not a lock.

## Pouch Capacity

A standard belt pouch holds 5 inventory slots.

Stackable items (Shards, crafting materials of the same type) occupy a single slot
per stack regardless of quantity — a stack of 100 Shards and a stack of 3 Shards both take up 1 slot.
Non-stackable or unique items (cards, cores, gear, quest items) each occupy their own slot.

## Crafting Costs by Card Rarity

Shard cost is set solely by the Core's rarity tier — Shards
themselves carry no rarity of their own:

- Common = 10 Shards
- Uncommon = 20 Shards
- Rare = 100 Shards
- Legendary = 1000 Shards
- Mythic = 5000 Shards

Canonical status/summary phrasing for a Core in progress: **"[Name]
holds a [Rarity] Core, X/Y Shards fueled."** Cores are identified on
pickup — rarity is known immediately, with no mystery/reveal-later
arc.

## Legendary Card Crafting Cost

A Legendary Card requires 1000 Shards fueled into a Legendary-rarity
Core assembled in the Legendary Card Factory Slot. Cores are
identified on pickup — there is no mystery/reveal-later arc; rarity
is known immediately.
Current known example: Aric Dalan holds a Legendary Core, 1/1000
Shards fueled, as of end of Book One. Per Aric's end-of-book character
sheet, this Core is held in reserve and is not slotted in the Factory
Slot.

## Base Recipe

Core + Shard fuel is the base crafting recipe. The Core supplies the
rarity tier (and therefore the Shard quantity required, per Crafting
Costs by Card Rarity above); Shards themselves are generic and carry
no rarity of their own.
