# Crafting

## Shards

All shards regardless of rarity are tri-purpose:
- Economy: spend as currency
- Crafting: use as card components
- Progression: consume for XP via Shard Cultivation

## Factory Slots

Every rarity tier has a dedicated Factory Slot:
- [Common Card Factory Slot]
- [Uncommon Card Factory Slot]
- [Rare Card Factory Slot]
- [Legendary Card Factory Slot]
- [Mythic Card Factory Slot]

Common and Uncommon Shards remain loose in inventory —
the player manually allocates them across the three uses.

Rare, Legendary, and Mythic Shards auto-sort into their
factory slot on acquisition for organizational purposes.
They can be withdrawn at any time and used for economy
or progression instead.

The factory slot is an organizational tool, not a lock.

## Legendary Card Crafting Cost

A Legendary Card requires 500 Legendary Shards assembled
in the Legendary Card Factory Slot.
Current known Legendary Card: Shard of the Titan's Aegis
(Aric Dalan, 1/500 as of end of Book One).

## Base Recipe

Core Shard + Fuel Shard is the base crafting recipe.
