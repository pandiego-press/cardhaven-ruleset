Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Mythic

## Installation Rules

Cards are installed into Card Slots. Starting Card Slot count is 3
(multiple characters begin with "[3 Card Slots Available]"). Filling
a slot triggers a 60-second Install Cooldown (confirmed via "[Card
Slot 1: Filled] [Install Cooldown: 60 Seconds]").

## Storage — Card Box / Sleeves

Uninstalled cards must be stored in a card box or sleeves.
Unprotected raw cards are volatile — wild magic held exposed
too long without being installed or secured can leak, causing
damage to the holder and to anything within a few yards of
them. A card box/sleeve is a temporary dampener, not a
permanent solution: cards must still be installed within a
bounded window after the pack is opened (see Progression) or
the leak eventually escapes containment.

## Death Mechanic

Cards fall into two categories on death:

- **Class card (Soulbound):** shatters on death. Not
  uninstalled, not recovered — gone permanently. No refund.
- **Slotted cards:** drop as loot on the body. Not guaranteed
  to drop as a complete card — depending on how the magic
  unravels, a slotted card may drop as a fully assembled card,
  as cores, or as raw shards. Whoever (or whatever) killed you
  gets to find out which.
