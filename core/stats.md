## Core Attributes

Known as the **Attribute Diamond** — four core stats drive all
derived resource pools:

- **STR (Strength)** — Focus Cap
- **CON (Constitution)** — Stamina, HP
- **INT (Intelligence)** — Mana
- **DEX (Dexterity)** — Agility

## Derived Pools (formulas pending confirmation)

- Focus Cap = STR-derived
- Stamina / HP = CON-derived
- Mana = INT-derived
- Agility = DEX × 2
